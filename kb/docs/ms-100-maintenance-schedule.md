# AMR-100 Maintenance Schedule

This schedule lists the preventive maintenance for AMR-100 robots. Record every task in teh maintenance log, with the date and your initials.

## Daily checks

1. Wipe the lidar window with a dry microfibre cloth.
2. Check that both emergency stop buttons pop out freely.
3. Look at the drive wheel tyres for cuts or flat spots.

#### WEEKLY

1. Clean the charging contacts on the robot and on the charging station.
2. Check the batery level after a full charge. A pack that no longer reaches 95% should be watched.
3. Remove shrink wrap and straps from the wheels.

**Monthly tasks**

1. Check the lidar cable connector on the sensor mast and push it in until it clicks. Vibration loosens this connector on the AMR-100 more often than on the AMR-200.
2. Check the bumper safety strip for damage.
3. Inspect the Wi-Fi antenna on top of the robot.

### Every 6 months:

1. Inspect the wheel motor brushes through the inspection window on the drive module. Worn brushes are the most common cause of error 42 on this model. If the brushes are worn below the mark, plan a wheel motor replacement (SP-1001) before the motor stalls.
2. Replace the drive wheel tyres (SP-1011) if the tread is below 3 mm.
3. Run a battery health test from the dashboard (Settings, Battery, Health test).

Yearly
------

1. Replace the lidar cable harness (SP-1005) as a precaution.
2. Check the charging contact plates (SP-1013) for burn marks and replace them if needed.
3. Have the vendor check the brakes and the brake release switch.

NOTES
- Check the parts for this schedule in the ERP at least two weeks before the planned date, because some parts have long lead times.
- Battery packs (SP-1009) are replaced when the health test shows less than 70% capacity.
