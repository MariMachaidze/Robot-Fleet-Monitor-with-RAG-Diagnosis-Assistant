# Operating Procedure: Return to Charger

## Purpose

How to bring a robot with a low battery back to its charging station. The charging stations are at the end of aisle 1.

## When to use

Use this procedure when a robot's battery is below 15%. Robots do not return to the charger by themselves.

## Equipment

- Handheld pendant (stored in the shift lead's office)
- Robot key

## Steps

1. Collect the handheld pendant and connect it to the service port on the back of the robot.
2. Turn the robot key to **Manual**. The robot stops its current task.
3. Use the pendant joystick to drive the robot slowly to the charging station at the end of aisle 1. Keep the speed setting on low.
4. Line up the charging contacts on the robot with the contact plates on the station. The gap should be no more than 2 cm.
5. Drive forward slowly until the contacts touch. The charge light on the station turns orange.
6. Turn the robot key back to **Auto** and disconnect the pendant.
7. Leave the robot on the charger until the charge light turns green, which takes about 90 minutes.

## Important

- Never send a robot with a battery below 15% on a long route. It may stop in the middle of an aisle.
- Do not try to use automatic docking. It is not reliable on the current firmware and can damage the contact plates.
- Only staff who have been trained on the pendant may use it.

## Problems

- If the charge light does not turn on, clean the contact plates with a dry cloth and try again.
- If the robot cannot be driven with the pendant, contact maintenance and leave the robot where it is.
